# MS Paint 稚拙插画 Prompt

## 用户认可的原始风格措辞

保留这段作为风格核心，搭配具体主体与构图，不只单独发送它：

> in the most clumsy, scribbly, and utterly pathetic way possible. Use same color, and make it look like it was drawn in MS Paint with a mouse. It should be vaguely similar but also not really, kind of matching but also off in a confusing, awkward way, with that low-quality pixel-by-pixel feel that really emphasizes how ridiculously bad it is. Actually, you know what, whatever, just draw it however you want.

最后一句表达随意感，不代表可以忽略用户指定的颜色、主题和信息。

## 新图模板

```text
Create an illustration of [SUBJECT] for a minimalist presentation.
[ESSENTIAL RECOGNITION DETAILS].
Composition: [PLACEMENT / NUMBER OF SUBJECTS / ORDER], generous white space,
all subjects completely visible. No text, labels, logos or watermark.

Draw it in the most clumsy, scribbly, and utterly pathetic way possible,
like it was drawn in MS Paint with a mouse by someone who cannot draw.
Shaky uneven outlines, broken disconnected lines, awkward proportions,
incorrect naive perspective, uneven double tracing, tiny jagged pixel steps.
Vaguely recognizable but also oddly off. Low-quality pixel-by-pixel feel.
Add small concrete details while keeping it amateur and unpolished.

Pure white #FFFFFF background. ONLY orange-red #E6532F drawing marks.
Thin sparse outlines. No black, no extra colors, no shading, no gradients,
no paper texture, no polished vector curves or professional icon styling.
```

## 照片 / 产品案例转绘模板

先查看参考图。提供参考图给图像工具，再使用：

```text
Use the attached image as a subject reference. Redraw [TARGET SUBJECT] as
an intentionally bad MS Paint mouse doodle for a presentation.
Preserve [KEY SILHOUETTE / EARS / TAIL / SENSOR / WINDOW / POSE] so the
subject remains approximately recognizable. Simplify the background away.
Do not reproduce photographic detail or branding text.

Use the clumsy, scribbly, awkward style: wobbly broken outlines,
misaligned details, naive proportions, jagged pixel edges and uneven tracing.
It should resemble the reference, but feel charmingly wrong and amateur.
Pure white #FFFFFF background. Only orange-red #E6532F marks.
No black, extra colors, shading, gradients, realism, labels or watermark.
Keep the whole subject visible and leave generous margins.
```

把案例图标注为“概念涂鸦”，不把它当作准确产品照片或结构图。

## 构图补充

- 封面：主物体配少量断续想象轮廓。例如箱子上方问号，周围四种动物的弱轮廓；问号可画，文字另排。
- 类型页：各类型独立、大小相近、顺序固定。将四张独立图放在各自文本列中，避免横条与文字错位。
- 流程页：保持同一对象外壳与视角；每一步只改变交互、光点位置或动作。
- 关系变化页：用距离、停留与节奏体现变化，而不是添加爱心或夸张表情。

使用当前可用的图像生成工具，不把 SVG 轮廓占位图说成已经完成的生成插画。对已有代码原生的简单图解，可以继续使用 SVG；保证风格统一与信息准确。
